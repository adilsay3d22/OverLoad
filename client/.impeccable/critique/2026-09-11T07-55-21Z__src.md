## Design Specificity Verdict

Split along an alarming line: reviewed screens are authored, the screens where the product happens are not.
Home, Programs, Progress, ExerciseDetail, Profile are genuinely specific. LogExerciseFocus — the screen the
product exists to serve — is five stacked shadowed cards of identical weight, the generic card stack the rest
of the app spent four passes escaping. Welcome became the centred-mark-on-pattern welcome its own contract
explicitly refuses, and never shows the product name.

Deterministic scan: 67 findings, exit 0 (advisory). 66 design-system-font-size across 19 files; 41 are
documented in DESIGN.md prose but absent from its machine-readable ramp (doc out of sync), 25 genuinely
undocumented (14px x15, 34px x2, 7px x1). One codex-grid-background hit — confirmed false positive, it is the
mandated 26px grid texture.

Visual overlay: injection succeeded, verified two ways (30 overlay divs; screenshot of amber boxes). 30
anti-patterns on /home, 27 undersized-ui-text at 10px — the deliberate label register, but the same 10px text
that fails contrast, so the findings compound.

## What's Working

- BlockMatrix is a product idea, not a component: a block's whole shape as one object, scaling down into
  ShapeStrip and up into the tab hero — the same reading at three zoom levels.
- Home's loading state ships labels and rules immediately, rendering only unknown values as flat bars at real
  type metrics, rather than skeleton boxes.
- ExerciseDetail: four figures on one ruled grid so they read against each other; direction by arrow and
  weight rather than hue, so it survives colourblindness.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Block matrix key contradicts the grid it explains. BlockMatrix.jsx:66-74 — isNext adds bg-accent while
planned's bg-surface remains in the class list; Tailwind resolves by stylesheet order and bg-surface wins.
Verified in pixels: up-next renders white-with-ring, identical to the "Started" swatch, while the key draws it
as solid lime. Fix: compute one state string, not overlapping booleans. -> /impeccable polish

[P0] The set commit is the smallest, least forgiving target in the app and fails silently. 32px circle, 6px
from two drag sliders; at 320px SetRow's 244px of fixed widths exceed the 240px available and the weight field
collapses. Empty weight gives a 400ms shake and a vibration — no message, no aria-invalid, no live region.
Failed writes surface as 12px text below the rows and the rest timer never starts. Fix: >=44px target, two-row
layout under 360px, role="alert" rejection, hoist save errors, queue failed writes. -> /impeccable harden

[P1] Inverted confirmation ladder. Log out (reversible) gets a sheet; reset-all-data gets two steps; deleting
an entire 8-week program gets one tap and nothing. DeleteButton swaps its label in place at the same
coordinates with no live region — a double-tap wipes the account silently for a screen-reader user.
-> /impeccable harden

[P1] ink-faint fails AA in global chrome on every route. Measured: .caps 3.33:1, TabBar inactive labels
3.48:1 at 10px. .caps is 6 sites in 5 files, but TabBar.jsx:22,27 puts the token in the navigation of all ~20
screens. DESIGN.md's own Don'ts forbid it. Fix: point @utility caps at --color-ink-muted, swap TabBar's two
text-ink-faint, retire ink-faint as a text token. -> /impeccable audit

[P1] The app asserts facts its data does not license. Home.jsx:152 marks sessions MISSED purely from weekday
index, so a new program shows three failures on first run while the hero says "Start session" for one of them.
Weight is unbounded while reps/RPE are clamped, so one typo put 545kg x 5 / Est 1RM ~636kg permanently atop
Progress with no correction path. -> /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

Casey (distracted mobile): mis-grabs the RPE ruler reaching for the 32px tick, and pick() auto-saves slider
moves on a checked set, silently rewriting a logged RPE. 8->20 reps is 3.4 window-widths of dragging. Rest
ends with navigator.vibrate only, which does not exist on iOS Safari — on iPhone the timer expires in silence.

Sam (accessibility): Sheet and RestTimer declare role=dialog aria-modal with no focus trap, initial focus or
restore; rest takeover has no Escape. BlockMatrix puts up to 56 gridcells in the tab order with no roving
tabindex. BodyDiagram encodes three muscle tiers as one hue at three alphas.

Riley (stress tester): at 320px the weight field collapses to zero. AddExerciseFlow has no .catch on two
fetches — kill the network and it shimmers forever. Switch units to pounds and Home still prints kg.

## Minor Observations

- Login/Signup do not use Screen and set a 26px gutter against the app's 22px.
- "Create Account" vs "Create account" — same action, two spellings.
- LogSessionSummary.jsx:76 still opens with a .caps kicker above its heading — the banned pattern surviving in
  the one screen nobody reviewed.
- Finishing a session rewards you with a white pill reading "Session complete." No volume, no PRs, no
  comparison — and peak-end says that is what gets remembered.
- 21 hardcoded hexes across 10 files; roughly two-thirds duplicate existing tokens.
- Two surface contracts describe screens that no longer exist; one was written after the build it documents.
