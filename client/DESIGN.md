# Design System: Overload

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Gym Chalkboard"**

Overload is a paper-white app with near-black ink and one electric lime. It reads
like a plan written on a board in a training hall: the numbers are large enough to
catch at arm's length, the labels that name them are small, tracked and uppercase,
and the only colour carrying a decision is the lime on the thing you are meant to
touch. The ground (`#fafaf7`) is warm off-white rather than pure white, so white
surfaces sit *above* it without needing a shadow to prove it.

Density is honest rather than airy. Rows are compact (14px vertical), gutters are a
single 22px inset, and the type ramp is steep — a 64px tabular numeral sits directly
above a 10px label with nothing between them. Depth is mostly tonal: a border for
things in a list, a soft shadow for things standing alone, never both on the same
element. Dark surfaces are a distinct material, always carrying a 26px grid texture
so black areas read as drafting paper rather than as a hole.

The system currently runs in two registers. Most screens compose in rounded cards
(26px) on the off-white ground. **Home** was rebuilt to carry hierarchy in scale
alone: no card, border, box or shadow on the route, structure held by hairline rules
and a single vertical spine. That is recorded below as Home-scoped. The rest of the
app has not adopted it, and that inconsistency is known and accepted, not an
oversight — do not migrate other screens toward Home without a decision to do so.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm off-white ground, near-black ink, a single lime accent
- One committed typeface (Outfit) doing display, body and label work
- Tabular numerals everywhere a number can change
- Bordered *or* shadowed, never both
- Dark surfaces always carry the 26px grid texture
- Motion is short, quintic-eased, and always reduced-motion gated

## Colors

A warm-neutral paper palette with exactly one saturated accent, plus a muted
diagnostic family that colours data (muscle groups, session types) and never
colours actions.

### Primary
- **Electric Lime** (`accent`): the app's only saturated colour and its only call to
  action. Primary pills, the selected toggle pill, the active-tab indicator, the raised
  session button, the progress-bar fill, text selection. Never the background of a
  passive surface, never a text colour.
- **Lime Deep** (`accent-deep`): the readable form of lime. Text links, in-progress
  counters, focus outlines and the input caret. 6.68:1 on the ground — this is the token
  to reach for whenever lime has to be *read* rather than tapped.
- **Lime Wash** (`accent-wash`): the hover ground for surface buttons, icon buttons and
  unselected toggle pills. Its only job is hover.

### Secondary
- **Muscle-group families** (`chest-*`, `back-*`, `shoulders-*`, `arms-*`, `legs-*`,
  `glutes-*`, `core-*`): seven wash/ink pairs. Fourteen exercise categories collapse onto
  these seven families, so a tag's wording is the category and its colour is the family.
  Session types (Upper, Lower, Core, Full Body) reuse four of these same pairs — they are
  not a separate palette.
- **Chart bar solids** (`bar-*`): saturated solid forms of the same seven families, used
  only for filled chart geometry where a wash would disappear.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (`bg`): every screen ground. The browser page behind the app column is a cooler
  grey (`#e8e8e6`) so the phone-width column reads as a device on desktop.
- **Surface** (`surface`): cards, inputs, tab bar, icon buttons — anything lifted off the
  ground.
- **Ink** (`ink`): all primary text, and the dark-card fill.
- **Ink Muted** (`ink-muted`): secondary text, captions, inactive labels. 4.92:1 on the
  ground; this is the smallest safe text colour in the system.
- **Ink Faint** (`ink-faint`): **legacy, below the project's own bar.** 3.33:1 on the ground,
  where the WCAG 2.2 AA commitment requires 4.5:1 at the sizes it is used. It remains in the
  token block because a dozen screens and the shared `.caps` utility still reference it. Do
  not use it for text on new work; use `ink-muted`. Home uses it nowhere.
- **Line** / **Line Strong** (`line`, `line-strong`): the hairline vocabulary. `line` is the
  default 1px divider and card border; `line-strong` is the heavier rule that opens a section,
  the Home spine itself, and input placeholder text.
- **Dark family** (`dark-bg`, `dark-card`, `dark-card-2`, `dark-line`, `dark-line-2`,
  `dark-muted`, `dark-faint`): the inverted material for dark screens and dark cards.

### Named Rules
**The One Lime Rule.** Lime marks the single thing you are meant to touch. Within a route's
content it appears exactly once. The persistent chrome — the raised session button and the
active-tab indicator — carries lime as well; that is chrome, and it is precisely why route
content may never spend a second lime.

**The Data-Never-Acts Rule.** Muscle and session-type colour identifies data. It never becomes
a button, a link or a primary surface. Lime never identifies data.

**The Muted Floor Rule.** `ink-muted` is the lightest text in the system. Anything lighter is
decoration, not text.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Outfit (400/500/600/700/800, with `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`)
**Body Font:** Outfit — the same face
**Label/Mono Font:** none. There is no monospace family in this project.

**Character:** One geometric grotesque doing everything, worked hard across weight and scale
instead of across families. Tight negative tracking at display sizes makes big numbers feel set
rather than typed; wide positive tracking at 9–11px makes the labels read as small caps rather
than as shrunken body text. Tabular numerals are the constant: timers, weights, reps and stats
never reflow as digits change.

### Hierarchy
- **Numeral** (800, 64px, 0.82, -0.045em, tabular): the target set on Home — `3 × 5`, `RPE 7`.
  The screen's loudest element, captioned by a 10px label with nothing between them.
- **Display** (800, 52px, 0.92, -0.04em): the Home hero — session name, "No program yet",
  "Block complete." Home-scoped; no other route uses 52px.
- **Headline** (700, 30px, 1.1, -0.03em): the standard screen title in `ScreenHeader`.
- **Stat** (700, 26px, 1.1, -0.03em, tabular): stat-box values, stepper values, the Home error
  headline.
- **Title** (600, 20px, -0.02em): section headings and empty-state titles.
- **Subtitle** (600, 15px, 1): row titles — session names, PR names, list items.
- **Body** (500, 15px, 1.5): prose, empty-state copy, input text. Wrapped at ~46ch in the
  desktop aside; row copy truncates rather than wrapping.
- **Body Small** (600, 13px): text links, trailing row values, stepper labels.
- **Label** (600, 11px, 0.12em, uppercase): the shared `.caps` register — the section label that
  sits above the card it names, and field labels.
- **Label Spine** (600, 10px, 0.14em, uppercase, tabular): **Home only.** A tighter, wider-tracked
  label register. It is a deliberate translation of a monospace label register into the committed
  face, because adding a second family was not authorised. It is distinct from `.caps`
  (11px / 0.12em) and has not been adopted anywhere else.
- **Tag** (700, 9px, 0.08em, uppercase): category and session-type pills only.

### Named Rules
**The One Face Rule.** Outfit is the committed face. A second family is not available to this
system — if a register needs to feel different, translate it into Outfit through size, weight and
tracking, as Home's 10px/0.14em label does.

**The Tabular Rule.** Any number that can change between renders is set in tabular numerals.
Weights, reps, RPE, timers, streaks, counters, week indices.

**The Scale-Alone Rule (Home).** On Home, hierarchy is carried by size, not by containers. The jump
from a 64px value to its 10px caption is 6.4:1 and is load-bearing — nothing may be inserted between
a value and its label to soften it.

## Layout

A single phone-width column, `max-w-[430px]`, centred on a cooler page ground; above the `lg`
breakpoint a marketing aside (`max-w-[520px]`) sits beside it and the column stays phone-width.
There is no fluid desktop layout — this is a phone app that is visible on a desktop.

Each screen owns its own scroll and its own padding: a 22px inline gutter (`.screen-pad`), 56px of
top padding plus the top safe-area inset (112px when the floating mini rest timer is present), and
108px of bottom reserve to clear the tab bar (40px when there is none). The bottom bar honours
`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.

Vertical rhythm is coarse: 22px between stacked sections in the standard card composition, 40px
between top-level groups on Home, 28px between a rule and the block it opens, and 14px of vertical
padding per hairline row. Rows run full-bleed to the gutter; nothing is indented except off the
Home spine.

**The Home spine.** Home hangs its whole composition off one vertical hairline at the left gutter,
with content inset 18px from it. State markers are centred *on* the rule, which is what makes seven
weekday rows read as one run rather than as seven items. This structure is Home-scoped and appears
on no other route.

**Motion.** Standard screens stagger their stacked sections in (`Stagger`, 50ms apart, 240ms,
`--ease-out-quint`, 14px rise). Home replaces that with a single authored moment for the whole route:
the spine draws down (`scaleY` 0→1, 620ms, `--ease-out-quint`) and nothing else enters. Both are
gated — Home checks `useReducedMotion`, and a global `prefers-reduced-motion` block collapses every
animation and transition to 0.001ms.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat, with a small and strictly assigned shadow vocabulary. Depth normally comes from tone
(white surface on off-white ground) and from a 1px `line` border. Shadows appear in two roles only:
a whisper under standalone surfaces, and a coloured bloom under lime controls that makes the primary
action feel physically raised. Dark surfaces take their depth from the 26px grid texture instead.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgb(20 21 15 / 0.05)`): flat cards and icon buttons. Barely there.
- **Raised** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px rgb(20 21 15 / 0.07)`): a card standing alone rather than in a list.
- **Lime** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 24px rgb(150 196 40 / 0.42)`): under the primary pill button.
- **Float** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 34px rgb(20 21 15 / 0.2), 0 4px 12px rgb(150 196 40 / 0.45)`): the
  raised centre session button, the only element that floats over content.
- **Glow** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 34px rgb(198 242 74 / 0.34)`): lime bloom for transient emphasis.

### Named Rules
**The Bordered-Or-Shadowed Rule.** A card is bordered when it lives in a list and shadowed when it
stands alone. Never both on the same element.

**The Grid-Texture Rule.** Every dark surface carries the 26px grid (`grid-texture`). A flat black
rectangle is not part of this system.

**The No-Container Rule (Home).** Home has no card, border, box or shadow anywhere on the route.
Anything that would have been a container is a rule instead. Home-scoped.

## Shapes

Generously rounded, on a radius ladder that maps to element size: 999px pills for anything you tap
that is not a card, 26px for cards, 22px for rows and standard inputs, 14px for small inputs, 12px
for the numeric set inputs in the log table, 6px on the focus ring. Corners are always uniform — no
single-corner or asymmetric radii anywhere in the build.

Borders are 1px `line`, used as the boundary of list-context cards and as horizontal dividers.
Hairline rules are the same weight and colour; on Home a heavier `line-strong` rule opens a section
and a `line` rule separates rows within it. The Home spine fades to transparent over its last 56px
so a short screen does not end in a hard terminal.

Home is the one place with a squared vocabulary: its action bar has no radius at all (54px, full
bleed to the content column), and its four session states are four shape-distinct 10px marks — a
filled lime square (complete), a rotated filled square in the session-type colour (in progress), a
hollow square outlined in the session-type colour (upcoming), and a 12px hairline dash (missed).
Rest days carry no mark, so the spine runs clean through them.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), full-width, 58px tall, 16px/700 label
- **Primary (accent):** lime ground, ink label, lime shadow; hover deepens to `accent-hover`
- **Secondary (surface):** white ground, 1px `line` border, ink label; hover fills `accent-wash`
- **Ink:** near-black ground, white label; hover `#1f2117`
- **Ghost:** `ink-muted` label, no ground; hover resolves to full ink
- **Disabled:** 45% opacity, shadow removed, pointer events off
- **Press:** every interactive element carries the `press` behaviour — 150ms ease on transform,
  background, border, colour and shadow, with a 0.98 scale on `:active`
- **Focus:** 2px `accent-deep` outline, 2px offset, 6px radius, `:focus-visible` only
- **Icon button:** 34–44px circle (36 default), white ground, `line` border, card shadow, hover
  `accent-wash`. Icons are Phosphor SVG at 17–22px.
- **Home action bar:** Home's single lime element is a square-cornered 54px full-width bar with a
  17px bold arrow, not a pill. Home-scoped.

### Chips
- **Category / session tags:** pill, 9px/700 uppercase at 0.08em, 11px × 5px padding, family wash
  ground with family ink text, never bordered
- **Toggle pills:** 12px/600, 14px × 7px padding. Selected is ink on lime; unselected is white with
  a `line` border and `ink-muted` text, hover `accent-wash`. An optional 6px colour dot precedes the
  label for chart-series selection.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner style:** 26px (`card`); rows and stat boxes step down to 22px (`row`)
- **Background:** white surface, or ink with grid texture for the dark tone
- **Shadow strategy:** see The Bordered-Or-Shadowed Rule
- **Border:** 1px `line` in the bordered tone only
- **Internal padding:** 12–16px for rows and stat boxes; section labels sit *above* the card at 11px
  caps with 14px of clearance, not inside it

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white ground, 1px `line` border, 22px radius, 16px × 14px padding, 15px/500 text;
  placeholder in `line-strong`
- **Focus:** border shifts to lime with a 3px lime ring at 45% alpha; outline suppressed
- **Error:** border `#e0a392`, message below in 12px/500 `#b8401f`; the field shakes (380ms) on a
  rejected submit
- **Set inputs:** the log table uses a denser variant — 12px radius, centred, 15px/600 tabular, paper
  ground, 9px × 4px padding
- **Error note:** `#fdf1ed` ground, `#f0d9d2` border, 22px radius, `#b8401f` text with an underlined
  retry action

### Navigation
- Four roots plus a raised centre action, in a white bar with a 1px top `line`, pinned to the bottom
  and padded for the home indicator. Tabs are a 22px Phosphor icon over a 10px/600 label; the active
  tab fills its icon, resolves to full ink, and grows a 3px lime indicator that slides between tabs on
  a spring. Inactive tabs currently use `ink-faint` — see the contrast note in Colors.
- The raised centre button is a 62px lime circle carrying the dumbbell glyph, floating on the `float`
  shadow. Mid-session it becomes the session clock with a progress ring; a 550ms hold pauses or resumes
  and fires haptics.

### Loading & Empty States
- **Standard:** shimmer skeletons — `line`-filled blocks at the card's own radius (22px default) with a
  white sweep every 1.4s.
- **Home:** skeletons are unavailable there, because with no containers there is nothing for a skeleton
  box to be. Home ships the labels and rules it already knows immediately and renders only the unknown
  *values* as flat square bars at the real type metrics they will be replaced by. Home-scoped.
- **Empty:** a 72px bordered circle holding an icon, a 20px/600 title, 15px/500 muted body capped at
  280px, and a single full-width action. Empty states are always honest — nothing is stubbed to look
  populated.

### The Spine (Home signature)
One vertical hairline at the left gutter with content inset 18px, running the length of the route and
fading out over its final 56px. Weekday rows hang off it; the four session states are marked *on* the
rule as four shape-distinct 10px marks (see Shapes). Session-type colour rides those markers, which is
how the week keeps variety without spending the screen's one lime. The spine drawing down is the only
entrance animation on the route.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, radius, shadow and easing from the `@theme` block in
  `src/styles/index.css`. Nothing downstream hardcodes a hex.
- **Do** reserve lime for the single thing the user is meant to touch, and use `accent-deep` (#4e6011)
  whenever lime has to be read as text.
- **Do** set every changeable number in tabular numerals.
- **Do** give dark surfaces the 26px `grid-texture`.
- **Do** choose bordered *or* shadowed for a card, based on whether it sits in a list or stands alone.
- **Do** use `ink-muted` as the lightest text colour on anything new.
- **Do** gate entrance motion on reduced-motion, and keep it to `--ease-out-quint` at 240–620ms.
- **Do** put the section label above the card it names, at 11px/0.12em caps.
- **Do** keep new routes on the incumbent card composition unless a decision is taken to migrate the
  app to Home's rule-and-spine structure.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use `ink-faint` (#8a8a82) for text. It measures 3.33:1 on the ground and fails the WCAG 2.2
  AA bar this product commits to.
- **Don't** add a second typeface. Translate the register into Outfit instead.
- **Don't** put lime on a passive surface, and don't let a data colour (muscle or session type) become
  an action.
- **Don't** put both a border and a shadow on the same card.
- **Don't** introduce asymmetric or single-corner radii; the ladder is 6 / 12 / 14 / 22 / 26 / 999.
